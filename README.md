## Torg Eternity DSR Terminal
This simple module allow GM to create Dramatic Skills Resolutions (DSR) on the fly for the torgeternity system (https://github.com/MetaMorphic-Digital/torgeternity) of Foundry Virtual Tabletop.

### Screenshots

#### Mission Form
<img width="538" height="710" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-14 at 13 57 44" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0d166d55-b083-40a0-9a24-6719b29f4f05" />

Launching the terminal, you'll be presented a simple form including a rich text editor to add the description of your DSR. Each step can be configured using the system native skills and attributes.
Once you filled your form, you have two options: 
- Send: This will send directly into the chat as a chat card your DSR. Each step configuration are transformed as Inline Enricher to allow your player to just "Click to test". The mission is also sent to the Archives
- Arhive: Send the DSR straight to the Archives without creating a chat card.

#### Mission Archives
<img width="537" height="710" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-14 at 14 00 51" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d2b88df2-0200-4d56-9e4e-01ff1cb16f66" />

Every mission sent or archives will be display here for you to either send them again in the chat or edit them by restoring them (useful if you made a typo)

#### Chat card
<img width="295" height="757" alt="Screenshot 2026-05-14 at 13 59 52" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/541c3906-e267-4286-96b2-bc5f403d603b" />

This is what will be display for your players (and you). Each step is transformed as a Inline Test Enricher. If a "Last Ditch Effort" is added, it will be displayed as a small insert at the end, useful if your player spent more than five turns resolving the tasks and want to try something heroic!
